# Limitations

- ClinicalTrials.gov records are submitted and maintained by study sponsors and investigators.
- Public records may be incomplete, delayed, or inconsistently updated.
- Registered enrollment may be anticipated or actual depending on record status.
- Location records may not represent every operationally active site.
- Dates may be month-level rather than day-level.
- The monitoring-priority score is heuristic and has not been clinically or operationally validated.
- No patient-level data is used.
- TrialPulse does not assess efficacy, safety, regulatory compliance, or probability of approval.
- Portfolio-relative thresholds change when filters or the underlying dataset change.
